TxnLab Quick Guide

How to Use the Simulator

  1. Load a preset from Scenario Library or build custom transactions in Transaction Workbench.
  2. Choose an isolation level (READ UNCOMMITTED through SERIALIZABLE) to control read visibility and lock duration.
  3. Use Step or Play in Round-Robin mode, or switch to Manual Scheduling and click individual transaction step buttons to craft exact interleavings.
  4. Inspect live locks in Lock Manager, resolve cycles in Deadlock Detector, and verify precedence graphs in Serializability Analyzer.
